Paltrin Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026

Paltrin L.L.C. (“Paltrin”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) provides the Paltrin One platform, a smart home and HVAC monitoring system designed to deliver insights into environmental conditions, energy usage, and device performance.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Name and email address (via Firebase Authentication)

Device & Sensor Data

  • Temperature, humidity, air quality, and HVAC-related metrics
  • Energy usage data from integrated devices (e.g., Emporia, SmartThings, Kasa)

Usage Data

  • App interactions, diagnostics, and performance data

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide real-time monitoring and insights
  • Display dashboards and analytics
  • Improve system performance and reliability
  • Enable and maintain integrations with connected devices

3. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell user data
  • We may share data with trusted service providers necessary to operate the platform (e.g., Firebase, AWS)
  • We may disclose information if required by law

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored using secure cloud infrastructure (AWS, Firebase, and database services)
  • Data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols
  • We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect user data

5. User Rights & Control

Users may:

  • Access their data within the app
  • Request deletion of their account and associated data

6. Third-Party Integrations

Paltrin integrates with third-party services (e.g., SmartThings, Emporia, Kasa). These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

Paltrin One is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Paltrin One platform constitutes acceptance of any updates.
